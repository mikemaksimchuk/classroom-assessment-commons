About the Commons

A shared resource for stronger classroom assessment

The Classroom Assessment Commons is sponsored by the National Council on Measurement in Education Classroom Assessment Committee. It brings together publicly available resources that can support informed, equitable, and practical assessment decisions.

Resources are screened and reviewed by the Classroom Assessment Commons Resource Review Subcommittee, a designated subcommittee of the NCME Classroom Assessment Committee. The Commons shares resources for discovery and learning; it does not rank, endorse, or promote individual resources over others.

How resources are vetted

  1. Submit or identify: A resource is proposed, cataloged with descriptive metadata, and held out of the public catalog.
  2. Review independently: At least three reviewers record their identity, review date, rubric scores, and comments.
  3. Publish responsibly: The Resource Review Subcommittee confirms that the review threshold has been met before making the resource public. An authorized expedited-review decision is documented separately when needed.

20-point review rubric

Each criterion is scored from 1 to 4. The rubric supports consistent discussion and documentation, rather than a public ranking of resources.

Alignment
Measurement principles, validity, reliability, and fairness
Practical utility
Actionable guidance and usable implementation support
Equity and inclusion
Attention to bias, accessibility, and diverse learners
Engagement and quality
Clarity, organization, presentation, and usability
Currency
Current evidence, terminology, standards, and context

Resource-sharing disclaimer

The Classroom Assessment Commons links to resources believed to be publicly available on the internet. Every reasonable effort has been made to provide accurate titles, attribution, descriptions, and links, but errors may occur. Inclusion is for educational resource discovery and sharing only. It does not transfer, claim, or imply NCME ownership of any copyright or other intellectual property, and it does not imply endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation. All rights remain with the respective authors, publishers, and other rights holders. If you identify an error, or if you are a rights holder and would like your work removed from the Commons, please email Mike@Maksimch.uk with the resource URL and pertinent details.

Professional judgment, privacy, and AI-enabled tools

Resources in the Commons are provided for information and professional learning. Users remain responsible for applying local professional judgment and for following applicable laws, policies, and agreements, including FERPA, COPPA, and institutional data-governance requirements. Before using an AI-enabled tool or sharing information with any external service, review its terms, accessibility, privacy, and data-handling practices. Do not enter personally identifiable student information unless your organization has expressly approved that use.
